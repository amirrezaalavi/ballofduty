Contents:

- `adventurers/*.glb` — five Adventurers 2.0 characters (Knight, Mage, Rogue, Rogue_Hooded,
  Barbarian), 23-joint `Rig_Medium`, one 1024×1024 atlas each.
- `adventurers/*.scn` — generated merged, shadowless, one-surface skinned scenes (see README).
- `animations/rig_medium_*.glb` — Character Animations 1.1 General, MovementBasic,
  MovementAdvanced and CombatRanged sets.
- `animations/kaykit_medium.res` — shared full clip library extracted from those sets.
- `animations/kaykit_upper.res` — upper-body filtered overlay clips.
